# REI-Ω∞ Real Frontier Roster Population v0

Status: candidate research extension; non-canonical; G6 remains OPEN.

## Purpose

Populate the comparative frontier with real, current, primary-source-backed challengers without pretending that heterogeneous systems are directly comparable.

The roster is evidence-first:

```text
PrimarySource
+ SourceDate
+ ExactIdentityOrVersion
+ CapabilityScope
+ AccessState
+ ReproducibilityState
+ ComparabilityReview
-> ROSTER_CANDIDATE | SOURCE_VERIFIED | COMPARABILITY_REVIEW | FROZEN_FRONTIER_COMPETITOR
```

A famous or impressive system is not automatically a frozen competitor.

```text
Fame != FrontierAdmission
SourceVerified != DirectlyComparable
DifferentDomain != SameArena
CrossDomainWin != UniversalSuperiority
```

## Cross-domain comparability guard

A challenger can be source-verified while remaining non-comparable.

```text
Comparable(A, B, Scope) requires:
  ScopeOverlap
  AND InputParity
  AND OutputParity
  AND MetricParity
  AND BudgetParity
  AND ToolParity
  AND HumanAssistanceParity
  AND EvaluationVisibilityParity
  AND VersionFreeze
```

If this fails:

```text
SOURCE_VERIFIED
-> COMPARABILITY_REVIEW
not
-> FROZEN_FRONTIER_COMPETITOR
```

No overall ranking may be generated by averaging unrelated domains.

```text
WorldModelScore + TheoremScore + HypothesisScore
!= UniversalIntelligenceScore
```
