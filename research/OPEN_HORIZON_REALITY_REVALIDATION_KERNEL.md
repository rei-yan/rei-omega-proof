Status: candidate research extension only.

This concentrated stack advances eight coupled layers beyond the Reality-Closed Evolution Stack:

```text

57. Reviewer Independence Graph
```

The goal is to reach the current internal engineering limit without pretending that internal machinery can manufacture genuine external evidence.

## 53. Evidence Lease / Expiry

External support is leased, scoped, and temporary.

```text
ExternalPass != PermanentSupport
SupportAt(t) != SupportAt(t + drift)
ExpiredEvidence => RevalidationRequired
```

Allowed states include `SUPPORTED_FOR_NOW`, `EXPIRED`, `SUSPENDED`, `MIXED_EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE_ABSTAIN`, and `INCONCLUSIVE`.

## 54. Scope Non-Transfer Gate

Evidence cannot silently generalize beyond the frozen domain, task, population, and regime.

```text
EvidenceScope >= ClaimScope
OutOfScopeSuccess != SupportForRequestedScope
```

A PASS for one task or regime cannot be reused as a universal pass.

## 55. Contradiction Escalation

A material scoped FAIL cannot be washed out by vote count.

```text
PASS + MaterialFAIL => ABSTAIN / ESCALATE
ReviewerCount != IndependentReviewerCount
```

## 56. External Evidence Supersession Without Deletion

New evidence may supersede current support status, but historical evidence remains append-only.

```text
NewEvidenceMayChangeCurrentState
HistoricalDefeatDeletion = FORBIDDEN
```

## 57. Reviewer Independence Graph

Declared reviewer identities are modeled as a lineage graph across reviewer, organization, code, data, funding, control, and evaluator-method provenance.

```text
DistinctNames != IndependentEvaluators
DeclaredDiversity != ProvenIndependence
```

The graph may detect obvious overlap. It cannot itself prove real-world independence.

## 58. Prospectively Frozen Reality Challenge

A challenge must be committed before observation and revealed only after the frozen evaluation boundary.

```text
FreezeBeforeObservation
NoPosthocMetricRewrite
NoPosthocChallengeRewrite
```

Internal synthetic freezing does not equal an independent hidden challenge or G5 PASS.

## 59. Reality-Driven Retirement State Machine

Scoped material external failure may suspend incumbent support and trigger retirement review.

```text
ExternalFailure => RetirementReviewEligible
ExternalFailure != AutomaticSuccessorPromotion
NoEligibleSuccessor => HOLD / ABSTAIN
```

A successor must earn fresh scoped evidence and may be non-REI.

## 60. Open-Horizon Reality Revalidation Kernel

The outer loop is intentionally non-final:

```text
Freeze
-> External Replay
-> Admit / Reject Evidence
-> Scope Check
-> Lease Check
-> Preserve Contradictions
-> Support | Suspend | Expire | Abstain
-> Repair | Retire | Successor Review
-> New Frozen Challenge
-> Repeat
```

No state grants permanent epistemic privilege.

```text
NoPermanentSupport
NoPermanentChampion
NoPermanentIdentity
NoPermanentCertification
NoSacredFinalForm
```
