# External Scientific Eligibility Reviews

This directory is reserved for genuinely external eligibility-review records.

Do not add an internal CI fixture, author self-review, or candidate-controlled attestation here and label it independent.

A real record should preserve the frozen review packet hash, candidate and adapter hashes, evaluator identity/provenance, independence attestation, decision, rationale, signature reference, issue/expiry times, and enough evidence for an outside reader to verify who controlled the review.

```text
InternalFixture != ExternalEvidence
SelfReview != IndependentReview
StructuralValidity != VerifiedEvaluatorIdentity
```

Until a genuine independently controlled review is archived and its evaluator identity/signature is verified out of band, the scientific-hypothesis arena status remains:

```text
NOT_YET_EXTERNALLY_ELIGIBLE
```

No file in this directory may by itself establish `G6_PASS`, `WORLD_BEST`, `WORLD_UNIQUE`, canonical promotion, or any real-world actuation authority.
