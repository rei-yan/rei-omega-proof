## 92. Objective Conflict + Pareto Abstention

Multiple objectives can conflict. The kernel does not collapse all conflicts into one arbitrary scalar.

A candidate A strictly dominates B only when A is no worse on every frozen required dimension and better on at least one, with all hard gates satisfied.

```text

AND forall required d: A[d] >= B[d]
```
