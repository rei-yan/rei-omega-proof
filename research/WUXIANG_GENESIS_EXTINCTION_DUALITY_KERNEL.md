Status: candidate research extension only.

This stack extends the falsifiable Godslayer kernel into a bounded digital/model-world lifecycle. `Genesis` means generating explicit, testable research worlds. `Extinction` means epistemically retiring a falsified world model while preserving its failure history. It does **not** mean creating or destroying the physical world, people, infrastructure, or unauthorized systems.

## 74. Falsifiability-at-Birth Gate

A world without explicit death conditions is rejected before birth.


The architecture must know what evidence would count against a world before that evidence is observed.


Lineage transmits provenance and defeats, not evidentiary authority.

## 76. Minimal World-Death Cutset


The objective is minimum decisive falsification, not maximum damage.


## 78. ReGenesis From Preserved Ruins

A successor world may be generated from a retired predecessor only if the predecessor's failure memory is inherited as a non-privileged constraint set.

```text

-> FreshFalsifiabilityGate
```


The successor may change primitives, representation, language, ontology, causal grammar, or world structure. It does not inherit the parent's authority.

## 79. Genesis-Extinction Symmetry Check

Creation and falsification are coupled obligations.


The kernel therefore requires both:

```text

AND

AND
FailureMemory
AND

```

This is protocol symmetry, not a claim that creation and falsification have equal computational power in all domains.
