The nine systems are a stable high-level skeleton, not a finite-depth module list.

Each pillar may deepen without a preset architectural ceiling through better evidence, stronger governance, retained failure history, adversarial falsification, compression, replacement, and structural recomposition.

```text

MoreDepth != MoreAuthority

CurrentForm != FinalForm
RealityVetoRemainsAbsolute
```

## 无相 / Wuxiang

`无相` means the current form is never sacred merely because it is current.

A current implementation, primitive, abstraction, model, rule, or composition may be compressed, replaced, retired, or recomposed when stronger evidence supports a better structure.

```text
NoSacredFinalForm
BetterAbstractionMayReplaceCurrentAbstraction
BetterSuccessorMayReplaceCurrentREI

```

## 无极 / Wuji

`无极` means evolution has no preset endpoint.

It does **not** mean literal infinite capability, omniscience, invulnerability, guaranteed prediction, or immunity to failure. It means the architecture does not predeclare a final ceiling on learning, correction, compression, revalidation, or depth.

```text
NoPresetEvolutionEndpoint
UnknownMustRemainPossible
FailureMustRemainPossible
RealityMayAlwaysVeto
```

## 九柱成轮 / Nine Pillars Become a Wheel

The long-horizon loop is:

```text
Absorb
-> Map Into Existing Nine Pillars
-> Candidate Isolation
-> God Proof / DeathEye Attack
-> Falsification / Counterexample Search
-> Reality-Bound Evidence
```

New knowledge should normally be absorbed through the existing nine-system interfaces rather than creating a tenth pillar merely because a new domain appears.

## 神座 / God Seat depth

God Seat is not defined by accumulating an ever-growing pile of rules.

Its depth may increase through:

- sharper authority boundaries;
- stronger arbitration under conflict;
- higher evidence thresholds where capability increases;
- better provenance and traceability;
- irreversible retention of relevant failure history;
- explicit rollback and abstention paths;
- resistance to self-certification and evidence laundering;
- better governance compression when rules become redundant.

The intended direction is **deeper governance, not rule-count inflation**.
